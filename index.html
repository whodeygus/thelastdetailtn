Good to know

Frequently asked

Do you offer full planning? +

We specialize in day-of (month-of) coordination and partner happily with planners for full-service requests.

What does a day-of wedding coordinator do? +

We build a precise wedding-day timeline, coordinate your vendors, lead your rehearsal, cue your ceremony and reception moments, and quietly solve problems on the day so you can be fully present.

How much does a day-of wedding coordinator cost? +

Day-of coordination with The Last Detail TN starts at $1,000. We tailor the scope to your venue, logistics, and guest count, and recommend the best fit when you check your date.

What areas do you serve? Will you travel? +

Yes! We coordinate weddings across East Tennessee, including Knoxville, Sevierville, Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge and the Smoky Mountains, plus Blount, Jefferson, Hamblen and Cocke counties. We're happy to discuss travel elsewhere.

Are rehearsals included? +

Rehearsal leadership is part of how we work, so everyone knows their moment, order, and timing. We'll confirm exactly what's included in your recommended package when you inquire.
